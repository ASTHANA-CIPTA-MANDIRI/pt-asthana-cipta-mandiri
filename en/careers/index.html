Contact Us
What documents do I need?

Your CV and cover letter as PDFs (max 2 MB each) are required. You can also add one supporting document — portfolio, certificates, or transcript — and a portfolio/LinkedIn link. Both are optional but very helpful, especially for design and development roles.

When will I hear back?

We review every application. If your profile is a match, our team contacts you by email or phone for the next step. You can follow your hiring stage any time on the Check Application Status page using the number from your confirmation email.

Can I apply for more than one role?

Yes. Send a separate form for each role so the right team reviews your application.

No role fits me — what can I do?

Send a general application. We keep your CV and review it whenever a matching role opens at Asthana, Reka Technology, or Orcha Journey.

How is my data used?

Your data and files are used only for recruitment and stored privately. See our Privacy Policy.

Did not find the right role?

Send your CV as a general application. We will reach out when a suitable opportunity opens.
