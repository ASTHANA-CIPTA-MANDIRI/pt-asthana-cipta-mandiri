{{p.nama}} ("we") respects your privacy. This page explains what data we collect through this website, what it is used for, and how we protect it, in line with Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection.

1. Data we collect

We only collect the data you send us yourself through our forms:

  • Contact form: name, email address, and message.
  • Job application form: full name, email, phone number, the position applied for, and your CV and cover letter (PDF).

When a form is submitted, our system also records your IP address and browser type to prevent abuse such as spam.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your questions, quote requests, or partnership enquiries.
  • To assess applications and contact you about the recruitment process.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent misuse of our forms.

We do not use your data for any other purpose without your consent.

3. Storage & security

Data is stored on our servers and can only be accessed by authorised staff. CVs and cover letters are kept in private storage that is not publicly accessible. Data is kept for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, and then deleted or anonymised.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. It may only be shared with our subsidiaries (Orcha Journey and Reka Technology) when needed to follow up your request, or with the authorities when required by law.

5. Cookies & analytics

This website may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand which pages are visited most. These services use cookies and collect aggregated usage data without identifying you personally. You can refuse cookies in your browser settings.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • request a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data, including withdrawing a submitted application;
  • withdraw your consent to the processing of your data.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date of the latest update is always shown at the top of this page.
