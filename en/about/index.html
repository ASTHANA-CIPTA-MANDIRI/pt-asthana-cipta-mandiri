Company Profile

Technology and tourism, done wholeheartedly

PT. Asthana Cipta Mandiri is a company working in technology and tourism, committed to delivering innovative, efficient, and trusted services.

We focus on information systems, IT services, and travel services in Indonesia. Through our subsidiaries, Reka Technology and Orcha Journey, we bring that expertise directly to businesses and travellers.
