About Us

Meet PT. Asthana Cipta Mandiri — who we are, where we are heading, and the values we hold.

Company Profile

Technology and tourism, done wholeheartedly

PT. Asthana Cipta Mandiri is a company working in technology and tourism, committed to delivering innovative, efficient, and trusted services.

We focus on information systems, IT services, and travel services in Indonesia. Through our subsidiaries, Reka Technology and Orcha Journey, we bring that expertise directly to businesses and travellers.

Vision & Mission

Where we are heading

Vision

To be an Indonesian technology and tourism group that is innovative, professional, and globally competitive in delivering the best solutions and services to society — helping businesses grow and creating memorable journeys.

Brought to life by our two subsidiaries Reka TechnologyDigital solutions for business Orcha JourneyMemorable travel experiences

Mission

  1. 1 Run our business with innovation, creativity, and professionalism.
  2. 2 Deliver digital solutions — websites, apps, information systems, and digital marketing — that help businesses grow, through Reka Technology.
  3. 3 Organise safe, comfortable, and transparent trips for travellers, schools, and companies, through Orcha Journey.
  4. 4 Provide quality services focused on customer satisfaction, combining technology and tourism to make them easier, faster, and more trustworthy.
  5. 5 Build a healthy, collaborative workplace with integrity, backed by good and transparent governance.
  6. 6 Contribute to economic and social development through businesses with broad benefits, including for MSMEs and local tourism destinations.
